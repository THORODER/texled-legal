# Privacy Policy — TexLED

**Effective date:** October 8, 2026
**Last updated:** October 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile application **TexLED** ("the App") handles information. The App is developed and published by **Toolcosoft** ("we", "us").

If you do not agree with this Policy, please do not use the App.

---

## 1. Summary

- The App **does not require an account** and does **not ask for** your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location.
- Your **settings and saved phrases are stored only on your device**. We do not receive them.
- The App shows ads through **Google AdMob**. Google and its partners may collect certain data (such as the **Advertising ID**) to show and measure ads.
- In the **EEA, UK and Switzerland**, we ask for your **consent** before personalized ads and you can change it at any time.

---

## 2. Information stored on your device

The App stores your settings and saved phrases **locally on your device**. This information:

- stays **on your device**,
- is **not transmitted to us**,
- is deleted when you **uninstall** the App or clear its data.

---

## 3. Information collected by advertising (third parties)

The App uses the **Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob)** and the **Google User Messaging Platform (UMP)** to display ads and to manage consent.

Through these services, Google and its advertising partners may collect and process:

- **Advertising ID** and **app set ID** (resettable/limited device identifiers);
- **device and app information** (model, operating system, app version, language);
- **IP address** and an **approximate (coarse) location** derived from it;
- **ad interaction data** (impressions, clicks) and diagnostic/performance data.

This data is used for **advertising, ad measurement, analytics and fraud prevention**. We do **not** receive this data in a form that identifies you. Google's handling of this data is described in:

- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- How Google uses information from apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

**Consent (EEA/UK/Switzerland):** before serving personalized ads, the App asks for your consent and offers a way to change your choices (Google-certified consent message). If you do not consent, you may still see **non-personalized ads**.

**Opt-outs (any region):** you can reset or delete your Advertising ID and opt out of personalized ads in your device settings (Android: *Settings → Google → Ads*).

---

## 4. Legal bases (GDPR)

Where the GDPR applies, we rely on:

- **Consent** (Article 6(1)(a)) for personalized advertising and related data processing;
- **Legitimate interests** (Article 6(1)(f)) for security, fraud prevention and non-personalized advertising.

---

## 5. Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Data collected by the advertising SDKs is shared with **Google and its advertising partners** as described above. We have no servers that store your personal data.

---

## 6. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to **access, correct, delete, restrict or object** to processing of your personal data, and to **withdraw consent** at any time. Because we do not hold personal data about you, most requests relating to advertising data should be directed to **Google**. You can:

- change your ad consent choices from within the App (when offered);
- reset/delete your Advertising ID in your device settings.

For EEA/UK users, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. California (CCPA/CPRA) users may have additional rights; contact us at the email below.

---

## 7. Data retention

Your local settings remain on your device until you uninstall the App or clear its data. Advertising data is retained by Google according to its own policies.

---

## 8. Children

The App is **not directed to children** under the age of 13 (or the applicable age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has used the App, contact us and we will take appropriate action.

---

## 9. International transfers

Advertising data may be processed by Google in countries outside your own, including the United States, under appropriate safeguards described in Google's privacy policy.

---

## 10. Security

We take reasonable measures to keep the App safe. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure.

---

## 11. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will change, and material changes will be reflected here.

---

## 12. In-app purchases

The App offers an optional **one-time in-app purchase** ("Remove ads") that disables banner and interstitial advertising and unlocks the Premium features permanently.

- Payments are processed by **Google Play Billing**. We **do not receive or store your payment details** (such as card numbers); Google handles the transaction and billing information.
- To remember your purchase, the App stores a **local flag on your device** (and Google records the purchase in your Google account). You can restore it on the same Google account.
- **Refunds** are handled by Google Play under its own policies.
